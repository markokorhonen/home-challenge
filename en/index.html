What is Home Challenge?

  • Every chore has a price. Log a finished chore and the money goes to your own account.
  • A child photographs the finished chore and a parent approves it before the money moves.
  • Heroes, powers, badges, the hero of the week and a race board turn everyday chores into a game.
  • Your family's data travels in your family's own iCloud. No ads, no tracking, no accounts.

Free to download. The one-time Family purchase unlocks everything for the whole family.
