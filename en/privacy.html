Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

In short

  • Your family's data is stored only on your devices and in your family's own iCloud. The developer has no servers and no access to it.
  • The app has no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs and no user accounts.
  • Parents decide what is entered in the app and can delete everything from within the app.

Who is responsible

Home Challenge is made by Marko Korhonen ("the developer"). Contact: markokor@gmail.com.

What the app stores

The app stores only what your family enters or does in the app:

  • Family: the family's name and currency (€, $, £, kr or points).
  • Family members: first name or nickname, role (adult or child), colour and order, adults' permissions (admin or approver), the chosen hero and the hero's own name, the chosen game board and music, and the weekly and monthly prize amounts.
  • PIN codes: adults' PIN codes are never stored as such. The app stores only a salted hash (PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256) from which the code cannot be read.
  • Chores: name, icon, price, how often the chore may be done, the daily challenge and challenge reservations.
  • Activity: completed chores, approvals and rejections (and who decided), bonuses, prizes and payouts (amount, date, method such as cash, bank transfer or MobilePay, who paid and when receipt was confirmed).
  • Photos of completed chores: a child takes a photo of a finished chore so a parent can check it. The photo is scaled down (800 pixels on the longer side) and saved only in the family's data, not in the photo library.
  • Purchase: whether the family has unlocked the Family purchase (yes or no).

Each phone also remembers a few settings that never leave the device: whose phone it is, music and Face ID settings, and lockout times after wrong PIN attempts.

The app does not ask for or store email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, dates of birth, location, contacts or advertising identifiers.

Where the data is

  • On the device: data is stored in the app's own database on the phone.
  • In your family's iCloud: if a parent turns on family sharing (Parents → Family sharing), the data is stored in the private database of that parent's own iCloud account (Apple CloudKit).
  • Shared only with people you invite: a parent can invite family members with an iCloud sharing link. People who accept can see and edit the family's data on their own devices. Nobody else can see it.

iCloud data is stored and protected by Apple under Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy. The developer has no access to your family's iCloud data, and no data is sent to the developer or to any third party.

No tracking, analytics or ads

The app does not track you, does not collect usage statistics, shows no ads and contains no third-party analytics, advertising or sign-in services. The app connects only to Apple services (iCloud and the App Store).

Camera, Face ID and notifications

Camera

The camera is used only when a child photographs a finished chore for a parent to check. On a device without a camera, a photo can be picked from the library; the app receives only the chosen photo. The app asks for permission, which you can withdraw in iOS Settings.

Face ID

An adult can choose to open the parents' area with Face ID. iOS performs the check and the app only learns whether it succeeded. Face data is never given to the app and never leaves the device.

Notifications

Notifications are local: the phone itself creates a notification when iCloud brings a change from the family (for example a chore to review, an approval, a payout or the daily challenge) and reminds you before a reserved challenge expires. Apple's iCloud signals changes with a silent push message. The developer sends no notifications and never receives device push tokens. A notification may show names, a chore and its photo, so you can control what appears on the Lock Screen in iOS Settings. The app asks for permission, which you can withdraw at any time.

Children

Home Challenge is made for families, and children use it under their parents' supervision. A parent creates the family, adds the children and decides what is entered. A first name or nickname is enough for a child. Chores a child logs go to a parent for approval, and only an adult with the admin role can make a purchase. The developer does not collect any data from children (or adults), because no data ever reaches the developer.

Purchases

The Family purchase is paid through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment and account details; the developer never receives them. The app only learns from the App Store that the purchase exists and marks the family as unlocked in the family's own data.

Deleting your data

  • The whole family: the admin chooses Parents → Admin → Delete all family data. The data is removed from this device and from the family's iCloud, and the sharing invitation is removed.
  • A parent who joined: Parents → Admin → Leave family. The phone leaves family sharing and the family's data is removed from that device.
  • A single member or entry: the admin can remove a member under Family and roles, and a single entry under All activity.
  • In iCloud settings: iPhone Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Home Challenge → Delete Data.
  • Deleting the app removes the data on that device. The family's iCloud data remains until it is deleted as described above.

Data is kept until your family deletes it. Because the developer has no access to the data, the developer cannot view, correct or delete it on your behalf. All of this is in your family's hands, in the app and in iCloud.
