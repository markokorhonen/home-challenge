Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Open the app and choose Create family. Give the family a name and currency, add members, pick heroes and chores with prices. Finally choose whose phone this is.

How do other family members join?

A parent opens the lock (top right) → Family sharing, turns sharing on and chooses Invite family member. Send the link, for example with Messages. On the other phone, open the link or choose Join a family in the welcome screens. Every phone needs an iCloud account.

Changes don't show up on another phone
  • Check that the phone is signed in to iCloud and has free iCloud storage.
  • Open the app: it fetches changes every time it opens. Failed uploads are retried automatically.
  • Make sure every phone in the family has the same, latest version from the App Store.
I forgot my PIN

The admin can reset another adult's PIN under Parents → Admin → Family and roles. If Face ID is turned on, it can also open the parents' area. After five wrong attempts the keypad locks for a while.

What is free and what does the Family purchase unlock?

The free version has one adult and one child, one game board, four heroes, up to five chores and ten completed chores a week. The one-time Family purchase unlocks every member, hero and board, unlimited chores and completions, weekly and monthly prizes, game music and the All-time heroes. One purchase covers the whole family, including children who are not in Apple Family Sharing.

I already bought it but still see locks

Open the purchase screen from any locked item and choose Restore Purchases. The buyer must be signed in with the Apple Account that made the purchase. Other family phones see the purchase once they have synced the family's data.

My child can't buy

That's by design. Only an adult with the admin role can make the purchase. A child sees a prompt to ask a parent.

How do I delete our data?

Admin: Parents → Admin → Delete all family data. A parent who joined: Leave family. More in the privacy policy.

Privacy

Your family's data stays on your devices and in your family's own iCloud. No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.
